About KMV Group

Crafting Iconic Skylines

Over two decades of precision engineering, technical innovation, and architectural mastery in UAE.

We are a premier Aluminum Composite Panel Fabricator, Installer, and Maintenance contractor operating across Dubai and Sharjah.

With over 20 years of hands-on experience, our team brings architectural blueprints to life through high-precision structural glazing, curtain walling, and bespoke aluminium systems.

Our solutions cater to luxury residential villas, commercial towers, industrial warehouses, hotels, and public infrastructure projects throughout the United Arab Emirates.

Vision

Setting international standards in facade design, structural glass, and cladding craftsmanship.

Mission

Delivering high-performance, weather-resistant, and aesthetically striking aluminium solutions.

Quality Standard

Rigorous quality control, ISO compliance, and robust safety protocols on every construction site.
