About Us

We are one of the leading Aluminum Composite Panel Fabricators, Installers and Maintenance companies in UAE.

We have over 20 years of experience in UAE and have executed many prestigious projects with quality services. We provide different types of services through our qualified and experienced team with complete client satisfaction.

Our team can carry out all types of Aluminum Cladding Works, Maintenance, Installation and other related technical works with cost-effective and high-quality solutions according to client requirements.

We provide high quality services for Residential Villas, Hotels, Hospitals, Schools, Commercial and Industrial buildings across UAE.

Professional excellence and integrity are the fundamental values of KMV Group & Ruknal Sahel Al Shamali Aluminum & Glass Cont. LLC, Dubai and Sharjah.
